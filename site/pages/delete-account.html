Roamkeep account deletion

Delete your Roamkeep account

Your Roamkeep account lives on your family's own server, not with us. This is how to delete it, and what goes with it.

In the app

  1. Open Settings

    Open Roamkeep and tap the cog at the top of the screen.

  2. Tap Delete my account

    It's at the bottom of Settings. Read what will be deleted, type DELETE and confirm.

That deletes your sign-in, your membership of your family's Keep, your location history and your check-ins from your family's server, straight away. Places you added stay with your family. It can't be undone, and the phone signs out.

Children

A member marked as a child can't delete their own account, just as they can't leave the Keep. In Settings → Delete my account, a child taps Ask to delete my account. The family's owners are told, and an owner deletes it. A child can always disconnect their phone meanwhile, in Settings → Disconnect this device, and the family sees that they did.

Owners

  • A child's account: in the Family tab, tap Delete account on their card. Requests from children also wait at the top of the Family tab.
  • Your own account: if you are the only owner and other people are still in your Keep, make someone else an owner first.
  • Everything at once: deleting your family's Supabase project deletes every account, place and location your family ever recorded, immediately. In Supabase, open the project, then Project settings → Delete project.

If the app says your server needs updating first

Deleting an account from the app arrived with Roamkeep 4.11.0. An owner can update an older family server from the app, in Settings → Family server. Until then, leave the Keep (Settings → Leave this Keep) and ask an owner to remove your sign-in in Supabase, under Authentication → Users. A child asks an owner to do both.

What we hold

Nothing. Your account, your locations and your history are stored only in your family's own database. The developer of Roamkeep has no copy and no access to it, and the relay that wakes phones keeps no record. So there is nothing for us to delete, and no request to send us.

Need help?

Email privacy@roamkeep.app. We can't delete anything for you, because we hold nothing, but we will help you find the right steps.
