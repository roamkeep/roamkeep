How it stays private

Your family's locations go to your family's database, and nowhere else

Most family trackers keep your household's movements on the company's servers. Roamkeep keeps them on yours. The only service we run for the app is a small relay that wakes phones with an empty message.

Your family's database

A free Supabase project on your own account, in the region you choose. You hold the keys, and you can delete all of it.

Families, not surveillance

Nobody is tracked without knowing

Roamkeep is for families who agree to share with each other. It is built so it can't quietly become anything else.

  • Everyone sees everyone. There are no one-way watchers in a family.
  • Adults can pause. For an hour, two, or the rest of the evening, and the family sees it's paused.
  • Always on show. A notification is up whenever location is being recorded, and it comes back if it's swiped away.
  • No quiet changes. If an owner marks someone as a child or changes what they can do, that person is told.
  • Owners approve new members. A forwarded invite is a request, not a way in.
  • Always a way out. Anyone can disconnect their phone, and the family sees that they did.
What you need

Five minutes, on one phone

  • Android phones for everyone who shares their location. There is no iPhone app.
  • A free Supabase account for the person who sets it up. Supabase runs your family's database for you, on your own account.
  • About five minutes on that person's phone. Everyone else just taps an invite.
