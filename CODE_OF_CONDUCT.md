# Code of conduct

Roamkeep is used by families, and the people who come here range from
security researchers to a parent who has never opened a terminal. Treat
everyone the way you would want your own family treated by strangers.

**Expected:** patience with people who are new to this; disagreement
about the work rather than the person; assuming good faith until there is a
clear reason not to.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults, or attacks of any kind; sexual
language or imagery; publishing anyone's private information, including a
location, an address, or a family's setup link; and pushing anyone, in any
way, towards tracking a person without their knowledge.

**Privacy is part of conduct here.** Don't post anything that would let
someone find or identify a real person, even your own family. Ask for it to
be removed if you see it, and the maintainer will remove it.

**Enforcement.** The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues,
and block accounts that break these expectations. To report something
privately, email [support@roamkeep.app](mailto:support@roamkeep.app). Reports
are read by one person and kept confidential.
