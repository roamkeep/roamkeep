Roamkeep is not an emergency service

Location is best-effort, and will sometimes be wrong or absent

Roamkeep depends on a phone, its battery, its operating system, its permissions and a network. Every one of those can interrupt it, and in normal use they do. A location may be stale, inaccurate by tens or hundreds of metres, or missing entirely because:

  • the phone is off, flat, in flight mode, or has no signal
  • location permission was declined, revoked, or reduced to “while using the app”
  • the operating system suspended or stopped the app to save power — Android does this routinely, and some manufacturers do it aggressively
  • battery optimisation is switched on for Roamkeep
  • satellite positioning is poor indoors, underground, in vehicles, or among tall buildings
  • an adult member has paused their own location sharing, which they are entitled to do at any time

The absence of an alert is not evidence of anything. If no “arrived” notification appears, that does not mean a person has not arrived. Treat what Roamkeep shows as a hint, never as a fact, and never as the basis for a decision that matters.

The notification relay

One small component is operated by us: a relay that wakes phones when a notification is due. It carries no content — see the Privacy Policy.

It is provided on a best-effort basis with no guarantee of availability. It may be rate-limited, interrupted, or withdrawn. When it is unavailable, notifications simply appear the next time the app is opened; nothing is lost from your database.
