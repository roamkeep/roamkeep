Before you start

The short version. Please read it.

🚑 Roamkeep is not an emergency service SOS alerts the people in your Keep and nobody else. It does not contact police, ambulance or fire, and no one is on call. In an emergency call your local emergency number (000, 911, 112…). Don't wait for this app.
📍 Location is best-effort, and will sometimes be wrong Phones go flat, lose signal and get throttled by Android. A position may be stale, out by hundreds of metres, or missing entirely. No alert is not evidence that nothing happened.
🤝 Everyone in a Keep must know they are in it Adults can pause their own sharing at any time. Never use Roamkeep to track someone covertly. A parent or guardian is responsible for anyone in the household who cannot agree for themselves.
🗄️ Your family's database is yours We do not run it, cannot read it, and cannot recover it for you. Roamkeep is free and provided "as is", with no warranty.

Set up your family

Your family's own server, in about five minutes

Roamkeep keeps your family's locations in a private database that belongs to you, not to us. You'll create it now, on a free Supabase account. Supabase is the company that will run the database for you, on your own account.

You'll need An email address for the Supabase account, and about five minutes, most of it waiting.
How it works You give Roamkeep a temporary key to your Supabase account. It creates the database, installs Roamkeep on it, then forgets the key, and at the end you delete it.
1. Create an access token On the page that opens, generate a new token:
  • Name it Roamkeep, and for Expires in pick the shortest.
  • Resource access: Organization, then select yours.Project, then select your family's server (phone setup names it roamkeep).
  • Permissions: Full access.
  • Tap Review access, generate the token, and copy it.
Only just signed up? Supabase asks you to create an organization first. Any name will do, on the free plan.
2. Paste it here Used only on this phone, only to talk to Supabase, and forgotten when setup ends. It is never saved.

Chosen from this phone's time zone. Pick somewhere close to home, because it's where your family's locations are stored.

    This takes about three minutes. Keep Roamkeep open. The screen stays on until it's done.
